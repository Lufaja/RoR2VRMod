Risk of VR 1.1.0 — Lufaja/RoR2VRMod
Experimental build: compiled against public RoR2 1.5.0 references.
Game launch and Steam Machine/Frame verification are still pending.

Read README.md and docs/STEAM-MACHINE.md before installing.
A GitHub source-code zip cannot be imported as a compiled mod.

After a successful source build, use build-tools/build.py package. Import that output
with r2modman: Settings -> Profile -> Import local mod.
Author: Lufaja; name: RiskOfVR; version: 1.1.0.
Dependencies: bbepis-BepInExPack-5.4.2122 and RiskofThunder-HookGenPatcher-1.2.9.
Only one com.DrBibop.VRMod plugin may be installed in the profile.

Launch through Steam/Proton with the generated profile options, SteamVR and the
Steam Frame stream running. The Steam Machine/Linux route is experimental.
Do not replace the Windows game plugins with Linux native libraries.

Keep the .vrmod-native backups under Risk of Rain 2_Data.
Close the game, disable the mod/patcher, then see tools/restore-native.py --help
for dry-run/explicit restoration. Do not remove backups to bypass a conflict.

Original mod: DrBibop; OpenXR continuation: Blowntobytes. MIT notices preserved.
